How the Ledger Appraises

i.

Read the page

An LLM performs structured extraction on the roaster's product page — origin, process, variety, price, bag size — into a stable feature schema, keeping verbatim source snippets for audit. Pages that aren't coffee products are recognized and declined, not force-fit.

ii.

Normalize the facts

Currencies convert to USD at live rates, bag sizes to grams, roaster country is resolved when absent. Every assumption the pipeline makes is recorded and disclosed in the appraisal record.

iii.

Predict & compare

Local rating and fair-price models — trained on a research corpus of reviewed specialty coffees — score expected quality and estimate what the coffee should cost, then judge the listed price against that estimate.
